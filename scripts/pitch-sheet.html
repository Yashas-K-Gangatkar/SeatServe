Why earlier attempts failed — and how we fix it
THE FAILURE
Cinemas never authorised outside runners.
THE NOTIFETCH FIX
The venue earns a commission on every order and formally authorises NotiFetch runners past ticket check. We arrive as your revenue partner — not a stranger carrying food.
THE FAILURE
Messy food in dark auditoriums.
THE NOTIFETCH FIX
Menus are restricted per venue: dry, theatre-safe items only, packed in sealed spill-proof boxes. Housekeeping rules are built into the product, not promised afterwards.
THE FAILURE
Stalls had no technology to take orders.
THE NOTIFETCH FIX
Each stall gets its own kitchen console — live ticket board, accept-to-ready flow — plus weekly settlements and a per-stall earnings dashboard. They say yes because it is their menu and their money.
