60-second guided demo

  1. 1Open “Customer · Seat F-12” (or scan a QR from the generator page).
  2. 2Add items from 2–3 different stores to ONE cart, then pay (mock UPI).
  3. 3You land on live tracking — each store has its own status ticket.
  4. 4Open the matching kitchen consoles — new tickets arrive with a chime.
  5. 5Advance each ticket to “Ready”, then deliver as the Runner. Watch tracking update in realtime.

Restores seed data: 1 mall, 2 cinemas, 6 screens, 4 stores, 2 sample orders.
