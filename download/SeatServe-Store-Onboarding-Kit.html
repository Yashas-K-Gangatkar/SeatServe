02 KYC checklist & commission terms

  • FSSAI license copy mandatory
  • GST registration certificate (or signed declaration) mandatory
  • PAN card of the business owner mandatory
  • Cancelled cheque or bank letter for settlements mandatory
  • Food Safety Supervisor certificate if held
  • Store logo (PNG) + 3 dish photos for the menu
  • Signed store agreement step 1
  • Commission 10–14% of item value, by category and fixed in your agreement, with no hidden charges.
  • Customer pays a 5% platform fee on top of menu prices; it is never deducted from your payout.
  • Gateway fee (~2%) is absorbed by the platform, not the store.
  • Settlements every T+2 to your bank account via Razorpay Route, with a per-order ledger.
  • GST on food is yours to remit; we invoice the platform fee monthly.
